# Selected kids questions: school

Selected from the three submitted batches: 150 rows, containing 111 distinct pairs before editorial review.

These 34 questions were chosen for clear wording, related choices, child appeal and variety. Some wording was shortened while preserving the original choice. Similar underlying ideas and weaker task preferences were omitted. The collection includes both thoughtful decisions and quick, playful preferences.

The CSV is ready for the existing importer. This selection has not been imported into the website.
